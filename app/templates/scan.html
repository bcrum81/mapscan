Paste a brand or search page if the dealer has one — e.g. bigdealer.example/s/your-brand or somedealer.com/collections/your-brand. That page is already scoped to your products, so the scan walks it (following rel="next" for pagination) and finds only what matters — a handful of requests instead of enumerating the dealer's whole catalogue.

Paste a bare domain instead and the scan falls back to reading their sitemap, filtering it to product pages, and matching slugs against your catalogue. That works everywhere but costs far more requests on a large retailer.
